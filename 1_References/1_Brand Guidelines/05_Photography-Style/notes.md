# Theme 05 — Photography Style
> Vision-verified 2026-05-31 — entries below reflect the actual section images.

The brand defines four distinct photography layers, each serving a different emotional and strategic purpose. Each layer is presented as a slide with a left text panel (label "Photography", a black sans-serif heading, grey bullet list, and an orange 👉 "Purpose" line) and a right-hand mosaic of reference photos on a light grey background with rounded-corner tiles.

## Layer 1: Product — Cars First
*(slide `02_product-cars-first.png`)*

**On-slide copy:** "1. Product (Cars First)" — Focus: The car as the hero. Bullets: Clean exterior shots (3/4 angles, front, side); Detail shots (lights, wheels, textures). 👉 Purpose: Showcase quality, trust, and desirability.

**Subject:** A single car (or a tight fleet) is always the unmistakable hero — no people in frame. Mix of compact SUVs, crossovers, hatchbacks and one sedan, across light (white/silver), dark (graphite/black), and accent (teal, blue, orange/red) colourways.

**Framing:** Mostly 3/4 front and clean side-profile views, car filling the lower-centre of the frame, parked or in gentle motion. Fleet/forecourt rows shot at a low oblique angle to emphasise depth and quantity.

**Lighting & colour grade:** Natural daylight and golden-hour. Several frames use warm low-sun light (highway overpass shot, blue hatchback) for glow and long shadows; others use even overcast/midday light for clean colour accuracy. Bodywork reads glossy with crisp highlight reflections. Grade is true-to-life and slightly warm, not stylised.

**Mood:** Sharp, premium, desirable, confident. The car gleams.

**What makes it on-brand:** Car is the focal point; aspirational but real Indian/suburban settings rather than sterile white cyc studios.

**Per-image inventory (right mosaic):**
- Teal/blue compact SUV (Tata Punch–type) parked on a paver driveway in front of a white flat-roof modern villa with greenery — hero shot.
- Forecourt row of light-coloured SUVs receding into the distance beside a warehouse — scale/choice.
- Blue hatchback/crossover photographed at golden hour, dramatic low warm light.
- White SUV parked under a covered concrete porte-cochère / driveway, soft shade.
- Graphite-grey crossover, clean side profile against a plain wall.
- Lineup of compact SUVs in orange, red and white parked at an angle — colour variety / fleet.
- Red/maroon SUV parked in front of a traditional curved tiled gateway (Korean/East-Asian temple-style architecture) — architectural backdrop.
- Grey SUV on a highway flyover/overpass at sunset, warm sky.
- White SUV on a quiet suburban residential street at dusk, warm window light from houses.
- Blue crossover on a rural country road bordered by dry grass and distant hills.

## Layer 2: Assisted Experience — Service in Action
*(slide `03_assisted-experience.png`)*

**On-slide copy:** "2. Assisted Experience (Service in Action)". Bullets: Inspection moments (agent + customer + car); Pickup/drop interactions; Doorstep service visuals. 👉 Purpose: Build trust in the process.

**Subject:** A Cars24 agent in a **royal-blue polo/uniform** together with a customer and a car. The interaction (inspecting, explaining, handing over) is the subject; the car is present and often open (bonnet up) but shares the frame with people.

**Framing:** Mid-shots and two-shots at human eye level — agent and customer either side of an open bonnet, seated together in the cabin, or standing beside the car. Candid, documentary feel rather than posed studio.

**Lighting & colour grade:** Predominantly natural daylight (outdoor driveways, forecourts) plus bright showroom interior lighting; one warm evening/doorstep frame lit by ambient porch light. Clean, true colour; the blue uniform and brand blue pop against neutral surroundings.

**Mood:** Warm, professional, approachable, trustworthy. The agent guides; the customer looks reassured and engaged. Reads as "we've got you covered."

**What makes it on-brand:** The blue-uniformed agent is the recurring trust signal; people are ordinary, relatable Indians, not glossy models. Service is shown as transparent and hands-on (open bonnets, tablets, paperwork).

**Per-image inventory (right mosaic):**
- Agent in blue polo and a customer inspecting a **red pickup/SUV with the bonnet up**, glass-front showroom behind.
- Agent and a customer (white shirt) reviewing a clipboard/document beside a **brown sedan** in front of a villa — doorstep/home visit.
- Agent seated in the **driver's cabin** with a customer, going over a tablet/checklist — in-car inspection.
- Agent standing beside a **red hatchback with bonnet raised**, presenting it.
- Agent standing arms-crossed and confident in front of a **solar-panel-roofed hub** with white/silver cars — facility + people.
- Agent inspecting a **red car with bonnet open inside a showroom**, blue-lit interior.
- Agent (woman in blue) and a male customer (red shirt) seated at a **consultation desk** with a screen — paperwork / deal moment.
- Agent and customer beside an **orange Tata hatchback with bonnet up at night/dusk**, warm doorstep lighting — doorstep service.

## Layer 3: Brand Lifestyle — Aspirational Layer
*(slide `04_brand-lifestyle.png`)*

**On-slide copy:** "3. Brand Lifestyle (Aspirational Layer)". Focus: Elevated lifestyle with Cars24. Bullets: Road trips, freedom, mobility; Urban + aspirational settings; Subtle brand presence. 👉 Purpose: Build emotional connection beyond transactions.

**Subject:** People living life through the car — families, couples, individual drivers, often with a dog. The car enables the moment; no agent, no overt branding. Emotion (joy, freedom, calm confidence) is the subject.

**Framing:** Intimate, editorial framing. Heavy use of **interior / cabin point-of-view** — looking out windows, from the back seat, across the front seats, and over the dashboard. Also exterior moments at open tailgates/boots and arrivals at home. Subjects mid-action and candid (laughing, waving, reaching out the window).

**Lighting & colour grade:** Warm, natural, "golden" light. Backlit windows and sun flare through glass; soft, alive skin tones. Grade is warm and lifestyle-editorial, never clinical.

**Mood:** Warm, joyful, free, human. Specific moments of connection rather than generic stock.

**What makes it on-brand:** The recurring windshield / window / back-seat POV ("the driver's seat is a place of possibility"), diverse relatable people, and dogs/kids signalling everyday Indian family life. Brand presence is implied, not stamped on.

**Per-image inventory (right mosaic):**
- Family with rolling luggage walking toward a dark car parked at a modern home — arrival / departure on a trip.
- Woman in a mustard top seated in the passenger seat, sipping from a cup, smiling — relaxed cabin moment.
- Several **children leaning out of a maroon car's rear window**, laughing — family joy.
- Young girl **waving an arm out of an open car window** beside an older woman, bright sky — freedom.
- Couple in the front seats with a **dog between them**, windows of greenery behind — road trip.
- Mature/grey-haired man driving, relaxed, with a dog and a child in the back — multi-generational.
- Family with a **golden dog gathered at an open car boot in a grassy outdoor spot**, luggage out — picnic/road trip.
- Two women seated in an **open rear tailgate** among trees, casual and happy.
- Confident woman in a denim shirt **driving alone**, hand on the wheel, calm self-assured expression.

## Layer 4: Hubs & Infrastructure
*(slide `05_hubs-infrastructure.png`)*

**On-slide copy:** "4. Hubs & Infrastructure". Focus: Scale and reliability. Bullets: Warehouses / car yards; Logistics operations; Inspection hubs. 👉 Purpose: Reinforce credibility and operational strength.

**Subject:** Cars24 physical retail and operations — the **vivid blue branded buildings with a green accent stripe and the white Cars24 logo**, forecourts of cars, and clean interior consultation/showroom spaces. The architecture itself is the brand asset.

**Framing:** Architectural exteriors shot 3/4 to show the building façade plus a forecourt of parked cars; wide forecourt rows for scale; and interior shots of consultation lounges and a single spotlit hero car. Daytime exteriors, controlled interior shots.

**Lighting & colour grade:** Bright daylight on exteriors so the saturated **royal/cobalt blue + lime-green** brand colours read at full strength against neutral paving and sky. Interiors are evenly, brightly lit; the final hero-car interior uses a clean white-walled space with focused spotlighting.

**Mood:** Credible, professional, at scale, dependable. The blue buildings function as brand OOH in themselves — instantly recognisable.

**What makes it on-brand:** The unmistakable blue-with-green-stripe hub architecture and white "Cars24" wordmark on every exterior; orderly fleets signalling volume and reliability.

**Per-image inventory (right mosaic):**
- Angular **blue hub building (green stripe + Cars24 logo)** with a black SUV parked in front.
- Hub **entrance with blue tiled steps** and people, "Cars24" sign above the doorway — retail footfall.
- Blue-clad **showroom with a lineup of cars (blue, grey, silver) parked outside** under bright sky.
- Large **blue tent/canopy hub** with white, red and black SUVs arranged on a paved forecourt — scale.
- Wide **blue glass-fronted showroom** façade with a digital totem/kiosk and potted plants out front.
- Forecourt **row of varied cars** with a row of solar panels behind — operations/sustainability.
- **Interior consultation lounge:** orange chairs, desks with monitors, a blue-lit "Find your drive" kiosk wall.
- Clean **white-walled showroom interior** with a single white SUV under spotlight — hero retail moment.

## Photography Do's and Don'ts (observed patterns)

**Do:**
- Use natural light; reach for warm golden-hour light on product and lifestyle frames.
- Cast diverse, relatable people — ordinary Indians, families with kids and dogs — not unreachable models.
- Keep the blue-uniformed agent present in every service frame as the trust cue.
- Show the inside of the car; use the cabin / windshield / window POV (the most distinctive device in the system).
- Keep the full car in frame and gleaming for product shots; let the blue+green hub architecture carry the brand in infrastructure shots.

**Don't:**
- Use generic stock imagery with no emotional specificity.
- Shoot overlit, sterile white-cyc studio car shots (the system favours real environments).
- Show people ignoring the car — they should be in relationship with it.
- Stamp overt logos onto lifestyle frames; brand presence there is subtle/implied.

## Creative Direction Insight
Cars24's photography vocabulary has three emotional registers: **desire** (the car itself, Layer 1), **reassurance** (the service, Layer 2 — anchored by the blue-uniformed agent), and **joy** (the life it enables, Layer 3). Layer 4 underwrites all three with **credibility** through the vivid blue hub architecture. The single most distinctive recurring device is the **cabin / interior point-of-view** — shots looking out through the windshield, from the back seat, or through an open window — which frames the driver's seat as a place of power and possibility.
