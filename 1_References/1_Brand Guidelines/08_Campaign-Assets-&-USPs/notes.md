**Colour & treatment (corrected from prior notes):**
- The badges are **NOT on a solid black background**. As presented in the guideline they sit on **white / light-grey tiles**. The badge artwork itself is **two-tone: black + bright leaf/neon green**, with green used as the energy/highlight colour and black as the structural/typographic anchor.
- Several full lockups place the green as a **highlight block or glow behind** the wordmark (e.g. Lifetime Warranty's "WARRANTY" sits on a green bar; Kavach+ and Easy Financing show a green halo/burst behind the icon).
- Wordmarks are **heavy, condensed, all-caps** — bold and stamp-like, more aggressive than the main brand voice. Numerals ("300+", "30") are oversized and act as the hook.
- Each mnemonic is delivered in **two forms**: (a) a small **icon-only stamp** (vertical lockup — icon over a tiny label) for tight placements, and (b) a **horizontal icon + full-text lockup** for primary use.

**To recreate a USP stamp:** build a bold black-and-bright-green emblem combining one hero element (shield / coin / oversized numeral) with a heavy condensed all-caps wordmark; use green as the highlight/glow and black as the type and structure; keep it stamp-like and high-contrast; provide both an icon-only and a horizontal text lockup version. Do not place on a black field by default — the source artwork reads as black+green on light.

## Campaign Template Language
Observed across brand materials:

### India Campaign Style
- Large, bold white text on full blue backgrounds
- Care Sans italic for emotional headline moments
- The "30 Day" offer is a constant signature — the circle/renewal motif reinforces the circular economy message
- Family and multi-person lifestyle photography is preferred

### UAE Campaign Style (Luxe Cars, Real Prices)
- More aspirational car imagery (Jeep Grand Cherokee, premium SUVs)
- "Under 100K AED" framing — speaks to the price-conscious premium buyer
- Car fleet presented together, clustered at bottom of frame
- Emphasis on the breadth of selection, not a single hero car

### Australia Campaign Style
- "Australia's First 30-Day Return Guarantee" — understatement as hero
- Clean, minimal template: a clean blue gradient, simple text, no product photography
- The message does the work, not the visuals

## Sub-Brand Campaign Tones

### Cars24 Luxury / Elite
- Cream/gold palette (see Color System — Luxury)
- Single car hero shot (premium vehicle, often silver or black)
- Understated, editorial typography — less bold, more refined
- No USP mnemonics — the luxury positioning must feel effortless, not promotional

### Cars24 Network (B2B/Dealer)
- More functional, information-dense
- Dark navy/black palette acceptable
- Numbers and specifics dominate (fleet counts, QC checks)

## Brand Personality in Campaigns

The brand purpose pillars translate to campaign types:
- **Empowering People** → Aspirational lifestyle campaigns; "your car, your story"
- **Driving Change** → Sustainability/circular economy messaging; tech-driven efficiency
- **A Greener Tomorrow** → CO₂ savings, pre-owned as conscious choice

Key brand taglines in active use:
- "Better drives, better lives." (master brand tagline)
- "Sell your car with ease" (service-led)
- "Take your time. Make it yours." (consumer reassurance — 30-day return)
- "Little bag for big journeys!" (product feature — Cars24 bag/merch campaign)

## Creative Direction Insight
Cars24 campaigns operate on two registers simultaneously: the rational (specific facts, verified quality, clear pricing) and the emotional (freedom, family, the road). The best Cars24 creative holds both at once — a specific offer ("30 days, no questions") wrapped in an emotional promise ("because the right decisions deserve time"). The USP mnemonics act like stamps of trust — they should feel earned and official, not decorative. The neon-on-black treatment is deliberately distinct from the main brand palette, making them stand out as "proof badges" within any layout.
