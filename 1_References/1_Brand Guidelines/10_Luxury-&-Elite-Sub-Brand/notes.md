# Theme 10 — Luxury & Elite Sub-Brand
> Vision-verified 2026-05-31 — entries below reflect the actual section images.

## What it Is
Cars24 Luxury and Cars24 Elite are premium sub-brands targeting high-value car transactions. The on-slide description reads: "The Cars24 Luxury and Elite palette is designed as a unified, premium color system that reflects elevated experience, trust, and high-value transactions." The visual identity is a deliberate, complete departure from the main brand's electric blue — a separate system built on warmth, restraint, and authority.

The slide states the palette expands beyond the core brand into a refined mix of:
- Warm neutrals and muted golds to express sophistication and aspiration
- Deep blacks and charcoals to add authority and depth
- Soft light tones to maintain clarity and balance

## Tone & Visual Language
- **Warm neutrals and muted golds** → sophistication and aspiration
- **Deep blacks and charcoals** → authority and depth
- **Soft light tones** → clarity and balance
- No brand electric blue anywhere in this palette — it is a clean, total break from the main brand
- The entire system is built from creams/golds (warm) + blacks/greys (neutral); there is no saturated accent color at all
- Typography on the slide itself uses a clean, restrained sans (headline "Sub-Brand colors: Luxury and ELITE" set tight, not heavy) — keep luxury type more refined and less bold than the main brand
- No USP mnemonics or promotional stamps — the luxury positioning must feel effortless

## Photography Style for Luxury
- Single premium vehicle as hero (silver, black, or white high-end cars)
- Clean, architectural settings
- Interior shots with leather, technology, ambient lighting
- Natural, muted, slightly desaturated colour grading (unlike the vivid lifestyle photography of the main brand)

## Logo Treatment
- The main Cars24 "C" mark is retained — brand recognition matters
- "ELITE" and "LUXURY" wordmarks use the Care Sans custom typeface
- Logo appears in black or white only within this palette — never brand blue

## Offline Sub-brand Application
- Cars24 Elite/Luxury showrooms use a refined material palette: stone, dark metals, warm wood accents
- Print collateral: matte, heavier stock (note the slide is explicitly tagged "COLORS – PRINT")
- Not the bold blue warehouse architecture — more gallery or premium retail

## Creative Direction Insight
The Luxury sub-brand is Cars24 in a different suit. All the same core values (transparent, trustworthy, customer-first) but delivered with the confidence of a premium concierge rather than a helpful advisor. Visually, the shift from electric blue to warm cream-gold is total — it signals a category change. When creating for Luxury/Elite, the rule is: **restraint is the luxury**. Every element that is absent (no loud color, no big badge, no claim stacking) is a design choice that communicates "we don't need to try hard."
