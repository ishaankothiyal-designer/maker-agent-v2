## Synthesized Guidance

### The Core Symbol
The Cars24 mark is a stylised open **"C"** inside a rounded square — designed to read as a circular economy, an opening gateway, and a key that unlocks possibilities (per the "Our logo" copy). Conceptually weighted, not decorative.

The "C" connotes: **C**ars24 · **C**ustomer-first · **C**ircular economy · **C**omprehensive solutions · **C**ommitment to exceptional service.

### Logo Versions
| Version | Use Case |
|---|---|
| Primary – Horizontal | Default, most commonly used. Headers, navbars, most creatives. |
| Secondary – Stacked (vertical) | Space-constrained or center-focused layouts. |
| Symbol only | App icons, favicons, very small formats. |

### Approved Colorways (exactly three)
- **Neo Blue** (`#4736FE`) — on white/light backgrounds *(hex from brand color system; not printed on this page)*
- **White** — on blue or dark backgrounds
- **Black** — on light/neutral backgrounds

Never: gradients, drop shadows, glows, color mismatches (see `09_logo-avoid.png`).

### Clear Space Rule
Minimum clear space = the height of the lowercase **"r"** from the wordmark, applied on all sides. Never press the logo to an edge.

### Logo Placement on Posts
- **1080×1080**: Top-left or top-right, ~260px wide, padding per clear-space rule.
- **1080×1920 (Story/Reel)**: Top left/center/right, ~300px wide; top unsafe zone = 225px, bottom unsafe zone = 420px.
- **Logo usage rule:** Whenever the logo is added, use the exact approved artwork from the `Logos/` folder in its intended form. Do not redraw, alter, or recreate the logo; follow the approved lockups and file versions precisely.

### Regional Logos
Twelve script adaptations: English, Tamil, Punjabi, Telugu, Marathi, Oriya, Bangla, Malayalam, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, plus Arabic. All keep the identical blue icon mark; only the wordmark script changes, "24" stays numeric.

### Sub-brand & Partner Lockups
- **Online services**: NewCars, Loans24, Insure24, Bikes24, CarTruth, Orbit
- **Offline (icon-prefixed)**: Cars24 Network, Cars24 Elite, Cars24 Luxury
- **Dealer apps**: Shield, Miles, Prime, Buyback
- **Partners** (Cars24 │ partner, vertical divider): Team-BHP.com, CarInfo, Vehicle Info
- Lockups supplied in **EPS and PNG**.

### Logo Typeface
The Cars24 wordmark and sub-brand lockups are set in the brand's **custom-made logo typeface (Care Sans — geometric sans-serif)**, reserved for logo units and signature moments only. Never use it for UI labels, body copy, or buttons; never stretch, condense, or add effects. (Body sans = Geist; serif headlines = Arapey — neither is used inside the logo.)

### What to Avoid (12 rules)
Distort · Miscolor · Drop Shadow · Disassemble · Low Contrast · Gradient · Add Effects · Stretch · Outline · No Extra Copy · Blur · Rotate. (Full verbatim instructions in the `09_` table above.)

### Creative Direction Insight
The brand hero language treats the mark as **alive**: the open "C" reads as a gateway a car can drive through. In motion/CGI contexts, lean into this "gateway through the C" metaphor — but the static logo itself must always obey the avoid rules (flat, solid color, upright, undistorted).
