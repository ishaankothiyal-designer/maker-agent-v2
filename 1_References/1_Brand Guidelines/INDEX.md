## Brand DNA

<a id="brand-story"></a>
### Brand Story
"Not just about cars — about moving lives forward. A car isn't just metal and wheels; for many, it's a ticket to independence, a better job, or a fresh start. We believe in simplicity, scale, and sustainability — making buying and selling cars easy, smart, and impactful."

📎 [06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/01_brand-story.png](./06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/01_brand-story.png)  
📎 [06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/02_brand-vision.png](./06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/02_brand-vision.png)

**Vision:** *Better drives, better lives.* — solving for Scale, Simplicity, and Sustainability.

<a id="brand-purpose"></a>
### Brand Purpose

Six principles that shape every piece of communication:

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Start with the driver** | Everything begins with who the customer is becoming, not just who they are |
| **Be their pit crew** | Stay in their corner from search to sale — never leave them alone |
| **Journey speaks** | The story belongs to the driver. We help — we don't steal the spotlight |
| **Say it right** | Trusted friend, not a salesperson. Clarity builds confidence |
| **Stay human** | Cars are about people, dreams, growth — bring empathy and joy |
| **Clear the road** | Remove friction. Every experience should feel like an open highway at dawn |

📎 [06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/03_brand-purpose.png](./06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/03_brand-purpose.png)

<a id="brand-persona"></a>

If Cars24 were a person: **Trustworthy** (confident, curious, knowledgeable) · **Approachable** (friendly, respectful, clear) · **Driven** (hardworking, smart, purposeful) · **Empathetic** (open-minded, supportive, inclusive) · **Collaborative** (helpful, humble, thoughtful).

Two approved gradients — both built from Brand Blue only. Always at **135° angle**. Never radial. Never introduce secondary colours.

| Name | From | To | Use |

| **Midnight Depth** | `#4736FE` | `#1A1270` | Hero sections, dark UI, primary buttons, campaign backgrounds |

## Typography

<a id="geist"></a>
### Geist Sans — Primary Typeface

Swiss-inspired sans-serif (Vercel × Basement Studio). Used for **all** body copy, headlines, UI, and captions.

| Weight | Token | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Light | 100 | Sublines, captions, supporting text |
| Regular | 200 | Body copy |
| Medium | 300 | Labels, secondary headlines |
| Semibold | 400 | Emphasis, UI elements |
| Bold | 500 | Headlines, primary messages |
| Black | 600 | Hero headlines, maximum impact |

<a id="care-sans"></a>
### Care Sans — Logo Typeface

Custom typeface exclusive to the Cars24 logo mark. **A signature, not a system font.**

✅ Use for: logo lockups · sub-brand name lockups · one signature campaign headline per layout  
❌ Never: body text · UI labels · buttons · repeated in a single layout · without brand context

<a id="type-weights"></a>
### Type Weights & Alignment Rules

- **Case:** Sentence case everywhere. Title case only for blog titles. Never all-caps.
- **Alignment:** Left-aligned or centred only. Never justified. Never right-aligned.

📎 [03_Typography/06_setting-type-alignment.png](./03_Typography/06_setting-type-alignment.png)

---

<a id="type-pairings"></a>
### Approved Weight Pairings

Hierarchy comes from weight contrast, not size alone.

| Headline weight | Subline weight | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Bold | Light | Standard — most layouts |
| Black | Medium | High impact, campaign hero |
| Bold eyebrow | Regular italic | Emotive, campaign-style |
| Medium italic eyebrow | Black | Product-led, specific claim |

Avoid: Medium/Regular or Bold/Light pairings.

## Brand Shapes

<a id="brand-shapes"></a>
### Shapes Overview

Two signature shapes encode brand values into every layout:
- **Trapezoid** (primary) — Speed, momentum, forward motion. Derived from car mirrors.
- **Star** (secondary) — Trust, discovery, celebration, excellence.

📎 [04_Brand-Shapes-&-Motion/03_brand-shapes-intro.png](./04_Brand-Shapes-&-Motion/03_brand-shapes-intro.png)

---

<a id="trapezoid"></a>
### Trapezoid — Primary Shape

Inspired by car mirrors (rear-view, windshield, side mirrors) — a frame that shows you the road ahead and behind simultaneously. **It frames. It implies momentum. It is never static.**

**Five-stage variation system:**

| Variant | Use |
|---|---|
| Basic Rounded Square | Starting point — UI cards |
| Base Form | Social media components |
| Softened Geometry | Reports, content panels |
| Applied Motion | Campaign layouts — slight tilt |
| Expressive Variation | Marketing storytelling — strong tilt |

Corner radius = always 1/10th of shape dimensions. Smooth flowing edges. No sharp corners.

## Photography Style

<a id="photo-product"></a>
### Layer 1 — Product (Cars First)

The car is the undisputed hero. **Purpose: desire and desirability.**

Shot types: clean exterior at 3/4 angle · front-facing · side profile · detail shots (lights, wheels, textures) · aspirational real-world settings (homes, roads, architecture) · fleet shots  
Mood: sharp, confident, desirable. Golden hour or aspirational settings. The car should gleam.

📎 [05_Photography-Style/02_product-cars-first.png](./05_Photography-Style/02_product-cars-first.png)

---

<a id="photo-service"></a>
### Layer 2 — Assisted Experience (Service in Action)

The human partnership. **Purpose: build trust in the process.**

Shot types: Cars24 agent (blue uniform) + customer + car · inspection moments · doorstep pickup/handover · expert reviewing paperwork  
Mood: warm, professional, reassuring. Agent present but never dominant — the customer's confidence is the emotional centre.

<a id="photo-lifestyle"></a>
### Layer 3 — Brand Lifestyle (Aspirational Layer)

The car enables life. **Purpose: emotional connection beyond the transaction.**

Shot types: families — multi-gen joy, road trips · solo drivers — quiet confidence · people + motion: windows down, sunroof open, dog out the window · urban settings with subtle brand presence  
Mood: warm, golden, alive. Specific human moments — not generic stock. The brand never competes with the story.

## Voice & Tone

<a id="voice-principles"></a>
### Core Voice Principles

| Principle | The rule | What to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Confident, not loud** | Clarity earns trust. Volume does not. | Exclamation stacks, ALL CAPS, shouting |
| **Warm, not familiar** | Knowledgeable colleague, not a party stranger trying too hard | Forced slang, over-familiarity |
| **Sharp, not clever** | Say something worth saying | Puns, wordplay for its own sake |

📎 [06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/04_tone-of-voice-principles.png](./06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/04_tone-of-voice-principles.png)

---

<a id="copy-rules"></a>
### Universal Copy Rules

✅ **DO:** Lead with what the customer gets · Back every claim with a number/fact/feature · Guide to a natural next step, never pressure · Be human — this is a big life decision

❌ **DON'T:** Make Cars24 the subject ("We offer…") · Use empty superlatives ("Best prices!") · Declare trust with adjectives — prove it with specifics · Use urgency/countdown language unless justified

📎 [06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/05_tone-universal-do-dont.png](./06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/05_tone-universal-do-dont.png)

---

<a id="tone-india"></a>
### Tone — India

**Affirming, celebratory, bold.** India carries the widest emotional register.

- Upbeat for wins · calm and solution-focused for problems
- Celebrate customer milestones with warmth
- Make the solution feel effortless — confidence from ease, not force
- Never mistake "positive" for "overhyped" — celebrate real things, don't invent superlatives

📎 [06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/05_tone-universal-do-dont.png](./06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/05_tone-universal-do-dont.png) *(India section)*

---

<a id="tone-uae"></a>
### Tone — UAE

**Sharp, specific, elevated.** Global audience (200+ nationalities), discerning, and ad-literate.

- Be sharp, not simplified — global doesn't mean lowest common denominator
- Set the scene; let the reader supply the emotion
- Considered word choices signal quality before the car enters the conversation
- Never prescribe emotions ("Feel the joy…") · Never assign identities ("Perfect family car!")

📎 [06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/06_tone-uae-australia.png](./06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/06_tone-uae-australia.png) *(upper half — UAE)*

---

<a id="tone-australia"></a>
### Tone — Australia

**Understatement, restraint, honest wit.**

- Acknowledge risk openly — honest reassurance beats false confidence
- Restraint earns more trust than enthusiasm
- Allow subtle observational wit where it fits naturally
- Run the Aussie filter: "Would an Aussie trust this?"
- Never loud, flashy, or self-congratulatory

📎 [06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/06_tone-uae-australia.png](./06_Voice-Tone-&-Copy/06_tone-uae-australia.png) *(lower half — Australia)*

| Rule | Correct | Incorrect |

| Oxford comma | Inspected, certified, and ready | Inspected, certified and ready |
| India prices | ₹3.5 lakh | Rs. 3,50,000 |

| Australia prices | $18,500 | AUD 18,500 / A$18,500 |

## Digital Composition

<a id="social-safe-zones"></a>
### Social Post Safe Zones

**Square 1080×1080 and Portrait 1080×1350**

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Logo | Top-left or top-right · 260px wide |
| Padding | Clear space = height of logo mark |
| Safe area | Centre + inner frame |
| Avoid | Centre-bottom unless intentional campaign style |

### Luxury & Elite — Full Override

Everything changes. Same values, premium register. **No brand blue anywhere in this system.**

**Palette:**

|---|---|---|

📎 [10_Luxury-&-Elite-Sub-Brand/01_luxury-elite-colors.png](./10_Luxury-&-Elite-Sub-Brand/01_luxury-elite-colors.png)

**Visual rules:**
- Single premium car hero · editorial lighting · muted neutral colour grade
- Arapey (serif) as typographic accent → see [#fonts](#fonts)
- Logo: black or white only. Never Neo Blue.
- No USP stamps. No promotional badges.
- More white space. Lighter type weights. Less is more.
- Copy: evoke, don't claim. No numbers, no superlatives.

**The rule:** Restraint is the luxury. Every absent element is a design choice.

## Creative Direction Principles

These sit above every rule. When in doubt, return here.

1. **The car enables life — not the other way around.** Every creative starts from the human journey.
2. **Clarity is the brand's superpower.** No noise, no clutter, no shouting.
3. **Blue is the brand's energy.** Electric, not corporate. Let it breathe — 60% is the rule, not the floor.
4. **The trapezoid is a lens, not a box.** It frames. It gives images forward momentum.
5. **The road is the brand universe.** Cars24 owns this lane. The CGI highway is a world, not a decoration.
6. **Restraint = luxury.** For Elite/Luxury, every absent element is a deliberate design decision.
7. **Specificity beats superlatives.** "300+ quality checks" always outperforms "best quality."
