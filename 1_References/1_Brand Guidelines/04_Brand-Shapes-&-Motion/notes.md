# Theme 04 — Brand Shapes & Motion
> Vision-verified 2026-05-31 — entries below reflect the actual section images.

The Cars24 visual identity rests on two signature shapes: a **primary Trapezoid** and a **secondary 4-pointed Star**. Brand attribute mapping (from the intro slide):
- **Speed & Momentum → Trapezoid**
- **Trust & Discovery → Star**

Consistent application of both forms across all channels is positioned as strategic (boosts brand recall, emotional connection, design cohesion), not decorative.

### `06_trapezoid-system.png` — Primary Shape / The Trapezoid System
The technical/construction slide. Headline "Primary Shape"; the shape is "a trapezoid with soft, rounded corners."
- **Corner radius rule (load-bearing):** corner radius is always set to **one-tenth (1/10 = 10%) of the overall size of the shape** — specifically "10% of Height & Width." This ratio is locked regardless of scale so the soft-structured character is preserved at any size.
- **Organic rounded geometry:** a custom rounded / soft-corner trapezoid; corner radius = 1/10th of the shape; smooth, flowing edges; **no sharp corners**.
- **Evolution sequence (left → right, 5 steps):**
  1. **Basic Rounded Square** — Starting Point
  2. **Base Form** — UI / Social media components
  3. **Softened Geometry** — Cards, Reports
  4. **Applied Motion** — Campaign Layouts (shape tilts / skews dynamically)
  5. **Expressive Variation** — Marketing, Storytelling (strongest tilt)
- **Proportion Flexibility:** three valid proportions shown — **Wide / Medium / Tall** — all retaining the trapezoid silhouette.
- **Do** (green checks): Maintain trapezoid silhouette · Use smooth rounded corners · Keep proportions balanced · Use brand gradients (also stated "Maintain 10% Radius," "Balanced Proportions," "Keep Trapezoid Silhouette").
- **Don't** (red crosses): Sharpen corners · Over-distort beyond recognition · Use sharp corners · Stretch unevenly · Mix unrelated styles · Break Core Structure.
- **Principle strip:** Consistent Core Shape · Flexible Variations · **Recognizable Identity**.
- Note: the trapezoid is drawn with the **wider edge at the bottom, narrower edge at top** (a stable, upright orientation) in the base form; tilt/skew is introduced only in the Applied Motion and Expressive stages.

### `08_secondary-shape-usage.png` — Secondary Shape usage rules
Headline "Secondary Shape." A labeled "Star" specimen (single brand-blue 4-pointed star with pinched concave sides) sits top-right; three application examples sit bottom-right (a black sedan "Warranty & Servicing Booklet" card with small accent stars, a brand-blue "Little bag for big journeys!" card with corner stars, and a brand-blue branded mug).
- **Correct Usage:**
  - Star-based rating systems (e.g., 4.9/5 stars)
  - Highlighting customer favorites, best-sellers, or trending deals
  - Enhancing emotional impact in festive campaigns, testimonials, or celebration moments
  - As a spark or flare accent in motion design for product unveilings or achievements
- **Incorrect Usage:**
  - Stretching, skewing, or flattening the proportions
  - Over-scaling, especially when it begins to distract from core content
  - Applying outer glows, heavy shadows, bevel effects, or gradients that contradict the brand's clean, flat visual style
  - Using too many stars in one frame, which reduces their symbolic weight

## Shape specs for image-gen / design prompts

**Trapezoid (primary)**
- Isosceles trapezoid, wider edge at the bottom, narrower edge at top, soft rounded corners (radius = 10% of height & width). No sharp corners. Flat solid brand-blue fill or brand gradient.
- Use as a **frame/aperture** that clips photography (mirror/window metaphor) as well as a filled panel.
- Allowed transforms: vary proportion (wide/medium/tall) and tilt/skew for motion — but always keep the recognizable trapezoid silhouette and the 10% radius. Never sharpen corners, over-distort, stretch unevenly, or mix unrelated styles.
- Connotations: speed, momentum, clarity, forward motion, informed decision-making.

**Star (secondary)**
- 4-pointed star with **concave (inward-curving) sides** and a pinched waist between points — flat, clean, brand-blue. Not a 5-point star; not straight-edged.
- Role: **punctuation / spark**, never the hero. Use for ratings, highlighting favorites/best-sellers, festive and celebratory accents, and motion sparkles.
- Keep proportions intact and flat: no stretch/skew, no over-scaling, no glow/shadow/bevel/gradient, and avoid clustering many stars in one frame.
- Connotations: trust, discovery, celebration, achievement, excellence, high performance.
