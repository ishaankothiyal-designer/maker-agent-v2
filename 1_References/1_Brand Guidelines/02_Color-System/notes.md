## `05_gradient-system.png` — Gradient System (Print page)

Intro copy: gradient system is built exclusively from the primary core hue; adds depth, dimension, hierarchy across digital and print while maintaining strict brand consistency.

### Primary Base
| Field | Value (as labelled) |
|---|---|
| Name | Primary Brand Color |
| HEX | `#4736FE` |
| RGB | 71, 54, 254 |

### Gradients
| Name | Tone tag | Start (labelled) | End (labelled) | Use note (on page) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Midnight Depth | DARK TONE | `#4736FE` | `#1A1270` | "for hero sections, primary buttons, and backgrounds where high contrast is required" |
| Ethereal Glow | LIGHT TONE | `#EBE9FF` | `#4736FE` | "Ideal for secondary UI elements, cards, and subtle background textures" |

### DO'S (green checks, on page)
- Use monochromatic shades for smooth transitions.
- Maintain `#4736FE` as the dominant anchor point in every gradient.
- Apply the "Midnight Depth" for high-impact hero sections and dark UI themes.
- Use the "Ethereal Glow" to add subtle depth to cards and secondary backgrounds.
- Ensure a 135° angle for a consistent, professional light source direction.

### DON'TS (red crosses, on page)
- Do not introduce secondary colors (orange, pink, or greens) into the gradient.
- Do not reduce the opacity of `#4736FE` to the point where it loses its brand vibration.
- Do not use harsh radial gradients; stick to linear transitions.
- Do not place low-contrast text over the transition mid-points.
- Avoid using pure black (#000000) for the dark tone; use the brand-tinted `#1A1270` instead.

## `06_color-usage-ratio.png` — Color Usage Ratio

Intro copy: to maintain a consistent, recognizable visual identity, Cars24 follows a structured 60/30/10 color distribution across all communications.

### Marketing graphics: online and offline — `60/30/10`
Bar diagram labels read **60** (large purple/blue block), **10** (dark indigo block), **30** (green block).
- 60% — Primary brand gradient (purple/blue)
- 30% — Secondary accent (green)
- 10% — Dark tones for contrast and depth
- Principle: "Creates a bold, high-impact brand presence across consumer-facing assets"

### Marketing graphics: Docs and Internal Coms — `60/30/10`
Bar diagram labels read **60-70** (light grey block), **20** (blue block), **10** (green block).
- 60–70% — Neutral base (white/grey)
- 20–30% — Brand gradient for highlights
- 10% — Accent (green) for emphasis
- Principle: "Ensures clarity, readability, and functional communication"

## Gradient System (quick reference)
Only built from the primary blue `#4736FE` — never mix secondary colors into gradients.

| Name | From | To | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Midnight Depth (dark) | `#4736FE` | `#1A1270` | Hero sections, primary buttons, high-contrast backgrounds |
| Ethereal Glow (light) | `#EBE9FF` | `#4736FE` | Cards, secondary UI, subtle background textures |

**Rules:** Always 135° linear angle. Never harsh radial gradients. Never reduce blue opacity until it loses vibrancy. Never put low-contrast text over the mid-point. Keep `#4736FE` the dominant anchor.

## Creative Direction Insight
Cars24 blue is electric, not corporate. The green accent is a visual "spark" — energy released, not decoration. The Luxury palette is a complete tonal shift — all warmth, no blue — and the palette change itself signals prestige vs accessibility.
